# HKSCS-2016 attribution

The file `data/HKSCS2016.json` is the publicly released “Hong Kong
Supplementary Character Set related information” dataset provided by the
Digital Policy Office of the Government of the Hong Kong Special
Administrative Region of the People’s Republic of China.

- Data URL: https://www.digitalpolicy.gov.hk/open_data/ccli/HKSCS2016.json
- data.gov.hk dataset page: https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-dpo-dpo_hp-hong-kong-supplementary-character-set-related-information
- Terms and conditions: https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions

Those terms permit free reuse of the data, including for commercial
purposes, provided that the source is cited and the intellectual
property rights of the Government are acknowledged.

This project cites the Digital Policy Office / data.gov.hk as the source
of the HKSCS-2016 character and Cangjie-code data, and acknowledges that
the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region owns the
intellectual property in that dataset. This note is an attribution, not
legal advice. The dataset is not a Microsoft product and is not taken
from any Microsoft IME private table.
